## Result levels

Candidate-expression decoding success is separate from unique adoption, real
execution, measured effect, same-A world reentry and continuous bilateral loop.
NOT_READY if internal adoption/connection is absent. NOT_TESTABLE if an otherwise
eligible fixed live run lacks environment/provenance. FAIL if a forbidden author,
selection, retry or source inconsistency occurs. Real loop PASS requires recorded
real inputs, an eligible complete expression, exact real request/receipt, renewed
observation to the same A and evidence of continued recursion. One perform alone
is never behavioral causal closure.

No formation/adoption law is invented in this preregistration. Do not filter the
original receipt-derived candidate by its provenance to force uniqueness.
