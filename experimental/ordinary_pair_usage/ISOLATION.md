# Isolation and semantic attribution

Version: `ORDINARY-PAIR-USAGE-TEST_ONLY-1`.

The original ReferenceEngine, EffectEngine, RealEffectEngine, their validation
records, and authoritative databases remain unchanged. UsageRealCore requires a
fresh explicitly initialized lifecycle or a database already marked with this
version. It refuses silent migration of previous experiment databases.

`UsageReferenceEngine._read_operative_operands` copies the construction hook from
`experimental/observation_interface_references/reference/engine.py`. It retains
the underlying TestOnlyFormalL0Engine ordinary traversal, exact reference
validation, connection/incidence construction, canonical identities, and complete
batch capacity check. Its intentional semantic difference is the historical Pair
wrapping predicate: accessibility and recursive support alone no longer authorize
a new wrapper. An existing Pair must be an explicit content operand returned by
the ordinary traversal. All such operands are processed; none is selected by
value, order, usefulness or available capacity. Previously saved content remains
stored and counted. Direct connection-backed canonical Pair reuse remains valid.

The experimenter supplies this generic constructor rule. It is not an internally
learned rule, a theory invariant, or an implementation of need/purpose/forgetting
from discussion constraint section 30. Positive explicit-content controls supply
their operand relations externally and do not establish autonomous formation of
those relations. No presently verified integration producer of such explicit
content-reference outcomes is claimed.

The unchanged EffectEngine ordinary hook dispatches through this new constructor
hook via inheritance. EffectRoute formation, occurrence effects, native projection,
receipt ownership and transport are reused without alteration. Offline renewed
experience evidence is separately labelled and is not new Minecraft execution.

The copied live recorder changes only its Core import. Fresh body/database,
external bootstrap, synchronized world-owner interventions, and actual raw
observations retain their distinct provenance. It cannot treat offline results
or synthetic receipt controls as live evidence.
