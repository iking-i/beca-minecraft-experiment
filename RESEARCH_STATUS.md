## 6. GSC candidate research branch

GSC-0, GSC-1 and GSC-2 are retained as valid **candidate-mechanism investigations**. Their local results are not deleted or downgraded, but they must not be promoted into the universal theory or treated as a completed generative core.

### GSC-0

```text
GSC0_PROTOTYPE = PASSED
```

Established within its TEST_ONLY finite model:

- selector-free structural closure;
- finite order independence under complete closure;
- idempotence;
- explicit conflict preservation;
- lineage/provenance preservation;
- formation of previously absent composite structures;
- honest `RESOURCE_LIMIT` when closure cannot be completed under the declared caps.

This establishes that **one specified structural-closure mechanism** can generate new canonical composite organization without choosing a winning candidate.

It does **not** establish:

```text
GSC = the generative law of Perfect Recursion
Closure = required universal recursion primitive
Composite = sufficient current organization
GSC resource exhaustion = A's current effective limit
```

The observed GSC resource exhaustion is currently a limit of this TEST_ONLY candidate algorithm/representation. It becomes evidence about an experimental individual's current effective limit only if such a mechanism is actually part of that individual's operative organization under a valid experiment.

### GSC-1

```text
GSC1_PROTOTYPE = PASSED
```

Established within its TEST_ONLY model:

```text
explicit linkage
+ complete GSC-0 closure
+ exactly one binding per required port
-> mechanically packaged complete outward tuple
```

The checker does not rank, select, repair, or fill missing/conflicting values.

It does **not** establish endogenous formation of the linkage itself or behavioral authorship.

### GSC-2

```text
GSC2_ROLE_FORMATION = UNRESOLVED
```

GSC-2 correctly exposed that, **inside the GSC-1 outward-port architecture**, explicit routing/linkage remained externally supplied and a clean first-generation role/linkage rule had not been identified.

This result does **not** establish that Perfect Recursion universally requires an `OutwardSchema`, a `Role(K,X,S)` representation, or a separate role-assignment mechanism. Those are properties/questions of this candidate branch unless independently derived from the broader theory.

## 8. Historical evidence policy

Do not delete or rewrite GSC-0/1/2, earlier audits, negative controls, or failed paths merely because the research interpretation has changed.

Use this distinction:

```text
historical experiment result
!=
current interpretation of its theoretical significance
```

## 9. Current forward rule

Before implementing another generative mechanism, first state independently:

```text
What observable change would count as a newly formed organization,
rather than merely:
- a new record,
- a new identifier,
- a new index/reference,
- a connected-set wrapper,
- an externally prescribed mapping,
- or a renamed policy?
```

Only after that criterion is explicit should a new mechanism be proposed and tested.

No present answer is assumed for the unresolved producer:

```text
reality + recursive history + current organization
-> ?
-> newly formed current organization
```

In particular, future work must not assume in advance that the missing mechanism is necessarily:

```text
Closure
Composite
OutwardPort
Role
selector
one-to-many propagation
many-to-one convergence
```

Any of these may be investigated as candidates, but each must earn its status through independent causal and counterfactual evidence.
